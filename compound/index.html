You are a place that hazards keep arriving at. You cannot stop them coming — you can only decide what you build, and how fast. Aiming and firing are automatic. Each time you level up, pick one card; measures that go together offer more. When the run ends, you can answer questions, and what you answer carries into the next run. WASD to move, SPACE to evacuate when something is already on top of you. That is the whole control scheme.

Risk is local. Choose where you are. The hazards, the order they arrive in, and which of the ten measures is worth taking first are not the same in any two of these.
