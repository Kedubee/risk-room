Risk Room

Playable prototypes about how disasters get prevented — and about the fact that prevention is invisible when it works. Each one is built around a real body of guidance rather than a quiz bolted onto a game, and each names its sources inside.

These are prototypes, not published material. They are internship work in progress, not an official publication of any organisation, and no organisation has endorsed them. Where a game cites a body of guidance, the citation is to that organisation's own published material and is named on the card or inside the game.

Everything here runs in the browser with nothing to install and no data collected. Third-party scenery is Poly Haven, CC0. Where a game leans on a body of guidance, the citation is inside the game and names the document rather than the organisation's reputation.
